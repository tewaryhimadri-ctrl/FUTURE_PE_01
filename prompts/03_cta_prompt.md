# CTA Prompt

Generate CTA sections for a local business website.

Create four variants:

1. Primary booking CTA
2. Consultation / discovery CTA
3. Local visit CTA
4. Final conversion CTA

For each:
- Heading: 4–9 words
- Supporting copy: 20–40 words
- Button label: 2–5 words

CTA requirements:
- One clear action
- Low friction
- Specific to the business
- Location can be used where natural
- Do not manufacture urgency such as "only 2 slots left"
- Do not promise availability unless supplied
- Do not use manipulative pressure
