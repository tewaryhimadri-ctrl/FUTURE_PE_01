# Homepage Prompt

Use the Master Prompt System first.

## Prompt

Create homepage copy for the supplied local business.

### Required sections

1. Hero
   - Headline: 6–12 words
   - Sub-headline: 15–28 words
   - Primary CTA: 2–5 words
   - Optional secondary CTA: 2–5 words

2. Short introduction
   - 50–80 words
   - Explain what the business does and who it serves
   - Use the business's actual differentiators

3. Why choose us
   - 3 benefit-led points
   - Each point: short heading + 20–35 word explanation

4. Featured services
   - 3 service cards
   - Service name + 25–45 word description
   - Keep details faithful to verified inputs

5. Local trust / relevance
   - 30–50 words
   - Mention location naturally
   - Do not fabricate reviews, ratings or awards

6. Final CTA
   - Heading
   - 20–35 word supporting copy
   - One primary CTA

## Style

- Human
- Specific
- Confident but not exaggerated
- Easy to scan
- Avoid clichés such as "unlock your potential", "revolutionary", "world-class" unless the client explicitly supplies them
- Avoid unnecessary adjectives
