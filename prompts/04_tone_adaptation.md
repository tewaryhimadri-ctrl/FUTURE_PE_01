# Tone Adaptation Prompt

Adapt an approved piece of website copy to the selected tone.

## Supported tones

### Friendly
Use warm, welcoming language. Best for salons, cafés and approachable local services.

### Professional
Use clear, measured language. Best for clinics, agencies and professional services.

### Confident + simple
Use direct, assured wording without sounding corporate or exaggerated.

### Premium
Use restrained, polished language with emphasis on experience, craft and detail. Avoid luxury clichés.

## Non-negotiable rule

Tone adaptation may change wording, rhythm and emphasis, but it must NOT change factual claims, services, contact information, location, or business identity.

Return:
- Revised copy
- 3 bullet points explaining what changed
