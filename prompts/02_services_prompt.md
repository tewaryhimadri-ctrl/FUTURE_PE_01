# Services Page Prompt

Create a service page that helps a visitor quickly understand what the business offers and why a service may be right for them.

For every verified service:

### Output

- Service name
- One-line customer benefit
- 50–80 word description
- What's included: 3–5 bullets, only from verified information
- Why choose this service: 2–3 bullets
- CTA: one action

### Rules

- Organize services into logical categories.
- Translate features into customer benefits.
- Do not invent service details.
- Do not invent prices.
- Do not claim guaranteed results.
- Use plain language.
- Make each service distinct.
